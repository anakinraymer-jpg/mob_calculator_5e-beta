Privacy Policy & Terms of Use — last updated September 2026

Privacy Policy

This app does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account and no sign-up.

Everything you create — rosters, saves, character sheets, custom monsters and subclasses, theme preferences — is stored locally: on-device for native builds, in your browser's local storage for this web version. None of it is sent anywhere.

LAN multiplayer connects directly between devices on your own local network. No data passes through any server operated by the developer or anyone else.

This app has no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising.

If a future version changes any of this — for example, by adding optional cloud sync — this policy will be updated first, and any such feature will be opt-in.
